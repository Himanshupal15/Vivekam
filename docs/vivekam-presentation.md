## Slide 2 — Problem and Proposed Solution

### Problem

- Young people face challenges such as stress, self-doubt, fear, and lack of focus.
- It can be difficult to connect historical teachings to present-day situations.
- Quotes shared online may be difficult to verify.

### Proposed solution

- Users choose a personal challenge or a teaching.
- Vivekam presents a modern scenario, an interpretation, and a practical challenge
  in a reel-style storyboard.
- The teaching's source citation and provenance are shown alongside the content.

## Slide 3 — Proposed Methodology

1. **Problem input:** User describes a situation, such as exam anxiety or
   low confidence.
2. **Teaching match:** The app suggests teachings using keyword and concept
   matching.
3. **Source grounding:** The selected catalogue entry includes source details
   and a link.
4. **Storyboard creation:** The app assembles a hook, modern scenario, teaching,
   interpretation, takeaway, and action scenes.
5. **Preview and export:** Users preview scenes and can export a script or
   captions.

**Integrity note:** Quote matching uses the local teaching catalogue; it does
not perform live source verification for each search. When Gemini is configured,
it can augment the content. Without a key, the deterministic templates provide
the fallback.

## Slide 4 — Alignment with Vivekananda's Ideas

- **Self-belief:** Encourage confidence in one's own potential.
- **Fearlessness and strength:** Face difficulties rather than retreat from
  them.
- **Education and character:** Emphasize personal development and action, not
  information alone.
- **Service:** Encourage care and responsibility toward others.
- **Authenticity:** Distinguish original teachings from fictional modern
  scenarios; do not attribute AI-generated text to Swami Vivekananda.

**Example:** “Have Faith in Yourself” → a relatable self-doubt scenario →
a practical 24-hour confidence action.

## Slide 5 — App Prototype: Demo Flow

1. Open the home page and select **Create Your Reel**.
2. Enter a personal challenge and choose a suggested teaching.
3. Select a story context and language.
4. Preview the scenes: dilemma, story, verified teaching, interpretation,
   takeaway, challenge, and source.
5. Open **My Reels** and demonstrate the script or caption export options.

**Current prototype limitation:** The app provides a storyboard/scene preview.
The project does not currently implement rendering or exporting a finished MP4
video. Present it as a reel storyboard prototype, not as a finished video
generator.
